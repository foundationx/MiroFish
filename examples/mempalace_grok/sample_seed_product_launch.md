## Lumora Labs announces the "Halo Kettle", a smart kettle with a subscription tier

**Portland, March 3, 2027.** Lumora Labs, a 40-person Portland startup that makes smart
kitchen hardware, announced the Halo Kettle today. It's a Wi-Fi gooseneck kettle that
brews tea and pour-over coffee to programmable temperature curves. It costs $149 and
ships on April 15, 2027. Pre-orders open today on Lumora's website and at the retail
partner BrightCart.

CEO Dana Okafor called the Halo Kettle "the first appliance that learns how you like
your morning." The kettle pairs with the Lumora app. It also offers an optional
"Halo Plus" subscription for $4.99 a month that unlocks 300 barista-designed brew
profiles, automatic descaling reminders and voice-assistant routines. Basic temperature
control stays free.

### Product details
- 1.0 L stainless steel body, 1200 W heater, ±1 °C accuracy.
- Hold-warm for up to 60 minutes; auto shut-off after 2 hours.
- Firmware updates over the air; Lumora promises 5 years of security updates.
- Made by contract manufacturer Shenwei Precision in Dongguan.

### Market context
Lumora's previous product, the Ember Scale (2025), sold about 38,000 units. It
received praise for its design and criticism for an unreliable Bluetooth connection.
Chief Product Officer Mateo Ruiz says the Halo Kettle moved to Wi-Fi "specifically
because of what customers told us about the Ember Scale."

Competitor KettleCo sells the non-connected KettleCo Pro for $99. KettleCo's founder,
Priya Raman, posted on social media this morning: "A kettle that needs a subscription
is a solution looking for a problem." Coffee influencer @BrewWithBen (210k followers)
got an early unit and called it "genuinely great hardware, questionable business model."

The consumer advocacy group FairTech Alliance said it would watch whether any core
feature moves behind the paywall after launch. It cited a 2026 case in which another
appliance maker disabled offline mode through a firmware update. Lumora's FAQ states
that "no feature available at launch will ever require Halo Plus."

### Funding and retail
In January 2027, Lumora raised a $12M Series A led by Northwind Ventures. Partner
Elena Brooks joined Lumora's board. BrightCart has an exclusive retail window until
June 30, 2027, and will feature the kettle in its spring "Smart Home Week" promotion.
